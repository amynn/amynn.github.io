Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

In short: your workouts and health data stay on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you choose to sign in with Google, a backup is saved in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only this app can open. We never sell or share your data.

Who we are

Trenna is a free fitness app made by an independent developer. Contact: amynnapps@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you log is saved in a private database on your device: workouts, sets, exercises, plans, snacks, objectives, quests, goals, body measurements, sleep you enter, hero progress, achievements and settings. Other apps cannot read it.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, the app can read (never write) only the data types you allow:

  • Steps: shown on the Hero and Health screens, used to complete step objectives and step achievements.
  • Sleep: shown with sleep stages and history, used for the sleep objective and the readiness score.
  • Weight: added to your body measurements.
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate: shown on your hero and Health screen, used for heart-rate zones during cardio and for the readiness score.
  • Respiratory rate and oxygen saturation: shown on the Health screen and used for the readiness score.
  • VO₂ max: shown as your fitness level.

This data is processed on your phone to show it to you and to power the app's fitness features. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold. The only copy that can leave your phone is your own backup (see below), and only if you turn it on. You can remove access at any time in Android Settings › Health Connect.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bluetooth heart-rate sensors

If you connect a heart-rate sensor (for example a chest strap or a WHOOP broadcasting heart rate), the app receives your heart rate over Bluetooth to show it live and calculate heart-rate zones. The app only looks for heart-rate sensors and does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in with Google, the app asks for:

  • your email address, to show which account is used;
  • access to the app's own hidden folder in your Google Drive ("appDataFolder"). The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.

The app then saves a backup of your data (including Health Connect data it has stored, such as steps, sleep and weight) to that folder so you can restore it after reinstalling or on a new phone. The backup goes directly from your phone to your Google account; we never receive it. To delete it: Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Trenna › Delete hidden app data, or sign out in the app.

Notifications and alarms

The app shows notifications for rest timers, workout timers and exercise-snack reminders. These are created on your phone.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No servers of our own: we don't collect or store your data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data on your phone. Delete the optional Drive backup as described above. Remove Health Connect access in Android Settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
